Time now is 8:50pm. I just hit the submit button for Apple’s review. Shag, but glad I’ve made it. Live Activity is coming!

## What’s New

> 🔔 Your bus is now just a glance away, even while you’re using other apps!

### Live Activity

- View bus timings on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
- Supports multiple buses, showing only the soonest arrival
- Automatically ends after arrival and sends a single approach alert

### Bus Route

- See full routes and other buses at each stop
- Quickly find alternative buses to your destination

### Other Updates

- Consistent swipe actions in Bookmarks and Bus Stops tabs
- Quick access to Bus Route and Live Activity via swipe left
- Refreshed map annotations and buttons
