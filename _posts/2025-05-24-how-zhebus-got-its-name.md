title: "How ZheBus got its name"
date: 2025-05-24 00:00:00 +0800
---

The initial name I had in mind for the app was **"Bus Buzz"**, but during the submission process, I realized that name was already taken. So I had to come up with a new one.

This is my very first app — it feels like my first “baby.” I wanted it to have a personal touch, something meaningful. Naturally, I thought about my son. I hoped to include a part of his name in the app so that one day he could proudly tell his friends, “My name is in that bus app — my Ah Pa built it!”

I started experimenting with different name combinations. I wanted something that included **part of my son’s name** and also clearly indicated it was a **bus app**. After trying out various options, one name stood out: **ZheBus**.

My son's name is **Yuzhe (宇哲)**, and interestingly, "zhe" (坐) also means "sit" in Hokkien. So when you combine "Zhe" and "Bus," it subtly suggests **“sit on the bus.”** It felt like the perfect fit — meaningful, culturally relevant, and uniquely Singaporean. As a Hokkien family, the name just clicked.

And that’s how **ZheBus** was born.
