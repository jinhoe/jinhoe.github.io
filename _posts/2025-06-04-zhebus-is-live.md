Many years ago, I casually asked a friend, *“Is it hard to build an iOS app without any programming experience?”* He laughed and said, *“Don’t bother — it’s way too difficult.”*

Well, today I’m proud to announce my very first iOS app, **ZheBus**, is now officially live on the App Store (Singapore only)!

And yes, he was right — it *is* hard. But thanks to the rise of AI and vibe coding, I was able to focus on the vision and let AI handle the heavy lifting. In this new era, you don’t need to be a Swift expert to bring an idea to life.

ZheBus isn’t trying to be the most feature-packed app out there. It shows real-time bus arrival times in Singapore — clean, simple, and distraction-free. No clutter, no fluff. Just what you need.

Well… except for the ads. I had to include them — my son’s got to eat 😅
