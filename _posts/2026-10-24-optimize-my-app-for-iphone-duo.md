I loaded my apps into the iPhone Duo simulator. They're still usable but look terrible. The challenge is that iPhone Duo has two screen sizes and two orientations, so technically I need to cater to 4 different ways of using the app. So much work! Imagine, for iPhone I only need to design for 1 layout.
