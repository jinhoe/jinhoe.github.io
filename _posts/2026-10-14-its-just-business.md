This morning I read an article about [a Singapore film that only 7 people showed up for](https://cnalifestyle.channelnewsasia.com/entertainment/sandbox-seven-viewers-singapore-films-cinema-support-590286){:target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"}.

Well, that is the reality. We can't make a film and hope people will support it just because it's a Singapore film. It doesn't work that way.

It's the same if you open an F&B and hope people will support it because it's Singapore food, or open a shop and expect people to buy just because the product is made in Singapore.

People buy things for a reason, be it good food or a reliable product. People watch films for a reason too: for entertainment, to laugh, to cry, etc. Wanting to support Singapore film alone is not reason enough.

Making a film is just like running a business.
