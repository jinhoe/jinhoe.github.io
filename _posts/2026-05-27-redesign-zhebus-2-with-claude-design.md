It has been almost a year since my last app update. This time the app update was triggered by Claude Design. So I was just playing with it, asking it to redesign my app. And I was so impressed by the first result it gave me, it motivates me to carry on. Also with the better version of LLM as compared to a year ago, I decided to jump in the rabbit hole again.

I ditched my signature bus icon, make the app cleaner. Also with close to 200 changes. It is actually a major revamped which is why I version it 2.0. The new LLM is really good, last time  when I build the app to test about 50% of the time will have some warning or errors. Now, it is about less than 5% of the time.

A year ago, these changes would have took me a few months to complete. Now I get it done in 2 weeks. And I am working only at night and over the weekends. So the speed of development is really amazing with AI.

Anyway although this app is mostly generated by AI, but it is orchestrated by me and with love. I really like the new design. I hope you like it too.
